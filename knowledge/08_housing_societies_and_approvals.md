# Housing Societies and Approvals

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. Not legal advice.

## Approved and unapproved societies

In Lahore, private housing schemes need approval from the Lahore Development Authority (LDA) or the relevant development authority. The LDA publishes lists of approved and illegal schemes. Buyers should check that the society and the specific block are approved before paying anything, because unapproved schemes can lack utilities, roads or legal title.

## Plot files versus possession

Some societies sell plot files before land is allocated or developed. A file is a right to a plot in the future, not a specific plot. Demo Realty only lists homes with possession, meaning the plot is allocated, the house is built and the owner can hand over the keys.

## Society no-objection certificate

Most societies require their own no-objection certificate (NOC) and that all society dues are paid before a transfer. Ask the seller for a dues clearance letter early, because unpaid development charges pass to the buyer in some societies.

## Building plan approval

The house should match the building plan approved by the society or authority. Extra floors or rooms built without approval can lead to fines, and lenders may refuse to finance an unapproved structure.

## Utilities

Ask for the latest electricity, gas and water bills in the seller's name. New societies may not yet have a gas connection, so some homes rely on bottled gas or solar.
