# Title Verification Checklist

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. Not legal advice. A qualified lawyer must confirm title before any payment beyond a refundable deposit.

## Documents to request

Ask the seller for the registered title deed, the previous chain of ownership, the approved building plan, recent property tax receipts, utility bills, and a no-objection certificate from the housing society where one applies.

## Checks a lawyer should perform

The lawyer should confirm that the seller's name matches the land record, that no mortgage or court order is registered against the home, and that the built structure matches the approved plan.

## Warning signs

Treat these as reasons to pause: the seller refuses to share original documents, the price is far below similar homes without an explanation, ownership changed several times within a short period, or a power of attorney is used in place of the owner.

## Apartments

For apartments, also request the building completion certificate, the sub-lease or ownership agreement, and a statement showing that maintenance dues are paid up to date.
