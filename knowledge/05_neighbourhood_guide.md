# Neighbourhood Guide

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. The neighbourhoods are real areas of Lahore, but every listing, price and figure in this course is made up for teaching. Commute times are to the city centre around Mall Road.

## Johar Town

Planned family area with good schools, parks and wide roads, close to Emporium Mall and the Expo Centre. Evening traffic on Canal Road toward the centre can be slow. Commute to the centre is about 25 minutes.

## Allama Iqbal Town

Established residential area with mid-rise apartment blocks around its markets, popular with young professionals and small families. Some low-lying streets see waterlogging during heavy monsoon rain, so buyers should ask about ground-floor and basement parking history.

## Model Town

Established central area with markets along Link Road. Houses are often older, so inspection matters. Street parking is limited. Commute to the centre is 10 to 15 minutes.

## Park View City

Newer gated community on the outskirts, quiet and green, with a commute of 35 to 40 minutes. Prices per square foot are generally lower here. Public transport is limited, so most households need a car.

## Gulberg

Dense central area along the Metro Bus route, with the shortest commutes in the city. Lower floors can be noisy from traffic. Well suited to buyers who do not want to own a car.

## DHA Phase 6

Planned, gated housing authority area in the east of the city, with wide roads, its own security and a strong resale market. Prices per square foot are among the highest in this course data. Commute to the centre is about 30 minutes, longer at peak hours. Most homes are houses on 10 marla or 1 kanal plots, and households generally need a car.

## Bahria Town

Large gated township off Canal Road toward the south-west, known for its parks, landscaped boulevards and private schools and hospital inside the gates. It is the furthest area in this course data from the centre, at 40 to 50 minutes. Buyers should check that the specific block is developed and has possession, since some blocks are still being built.

## Wapda Town

Established, mostly residential area near Johar Town with schools, mosques and local markets. Plots are mid-sized and the area is quieter than Johar Town. Commute to the centre is about 30 minutes. Some older streets have narrower roads, so buyers with larger cars should check access and parking.

## DHA Phase 5

Established phase of the Defence Housing Authority, between DHA Phase 6 and the older phases, with mature trees, parks and commercial areas close by. Most homes are 10 marla, 1 kanal and 2 kanal houses, and some apartments sit near its commercial zone. Security and gated entry are similar to DHA Phase 6. Commute to the centre is about 25 minutes.

## Lahore Cantt

Older cantonment area with wide, tree-lined roads, large plots and some of the shortest commutes to the centre, about 15 minutes. Many homes are 1 to 4 kanal bungalows on mature plots, so inspection of older structures matters. Parts of the area are near the Metro and main arteries. Some properties are on leased cantonment land, so buyers should check the title type with a lawyer before paying any deposit.

## Lake City

Planned gated community off Raiwind Road with a golf course, parks and newer drainage. It has 5 marla to 2 kanal houses, townhouses and apartment blocks. Commute to the centre is about 40 minutes. Buyers should confirm that the block has possession and utilities, since some blocks are still being developed.

## Farmhouse belts: Bedian Road, Barki Road and Raiwind Road

Farmhouses on the city edge sit on 2 to 10 kanal plots with a main house, lawns, fruit trees and often a pool or a tube well. Bedian Road is the most sought-after belt and is close to DHA. Barki Road is quieter and slightly cheaper. Raiwind Road offers the largest plots for the lowest price per square foot. Commutes are 40 to 50 minutes and a car is essential. Before buying, check that the land use is approved for residential farmhouses, that the access road is public, and whether water comes from a tube well or a society supply. Electricity and gas connections can take longer than in town.

## Everyday amenities by area

Johar Town, Wapda Town and Bahria Town have the largest number of schools close to homes. Gulberg and Model Town have the most shops and restaurants within walking distance. Gulberg is the only area in this guide on the Metro Bus route. DHA Phase 6 and Bahria Town have private security and gated entry. Park View City and Bahria Town have the most green space per home but the longest commutes.

## Flooding and waterlogging

Heavy monsoon rain causes street waterlogging in parts of Allama Iqbal Town and older parts of Model Town. Newer planned areas such as DHA Phase 6, Bahria Town and Park View City have newer drainage. Buyers should ask neighbours about water on the street during the last monsoon and check ground-floor walls for damp lines.
