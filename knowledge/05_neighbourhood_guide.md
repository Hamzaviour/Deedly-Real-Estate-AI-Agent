# Neighbourhood Guide

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. The neighbourhoods are real areas of Lahore, but every listing, price and figure in this course is made up for teaching. Commute times are to the city centre around Mall Road.

## Johar Town

Planned family area with good schools, parks and wide roads, close to Emporium Mall and the Expo Centre. Evening traffic on Canal Road toward the centre can be slow. Commute to the centre is about 25 minutes.

## Allama Iqbal Town

Established residential area with mid-rise apartment blocks around its markets, popular with young professionals and small families. Some low-lying streets see waterlogging during heavy monsoon rain, so buyers should ask about ground-floor and basement parking history.

## Model Town

Established central area with markets along Link Road. Houses are often older, so inspection matters. Street parking is limited. Commute to the centre is 10 to 15 minutes.

## Park View City

Newer gated community on the outskirts, quiet and green, with a commute of 35 to 40 minutes. Prices per square foot are generally lower here. Public transport is limited, so most households need a car.

## Gulberg

Dense central area along the Metro Bus route, with the shortest commutes in the city. Lower floors can be noisy from traffic. Well suited to buyers who do not want to own a car.
