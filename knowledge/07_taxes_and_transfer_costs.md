# Taxes and Transfer Costs

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. Rates below are illustrative teaching figures, not current law. Tax rates in Pakistan change with each federal and provincial budget, so a lawyer or tax adviser must confirm the actual rates before a transfer.

## What the 6 percent covers

The financing guide asks buyers to keep 6 percent of the price aside. In this course that covers stamp duty, registration and transfer fees, the buyer's advance tax, legal fees, an inspection and moving costs. For an expensive home or a buyer who is not on the active taxpayer list, the real total can be higher.

## Stamp duty and registration

Provincial stamp duty is charged on the value of the property when the sale deed is registered. This course uses an illustrative 1 percent for stamp duty and about 1 percent for registration and transfer fees. Housing societies also charge their own transfer fee.

## Advance tax on purchase

The federal government collects an advance tax from the buyer at transfer. The rate is much higher for people who are not on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) than for filers. Buyers should check their ATL status before agreeing a price, because it changes the cash needed at transfer.

## Declared value and valuation tables

Taxes are charged on the higher of the declared price and the government valuation table for that area. Declaring a price lower than the real price to reduce tax is illegal and can create problems at resale and with a lender's valuation.

## Seller costs

The seller normally pays the capital gains tax and the seller's advance tax. The sale agreement should say clearly which party pays each cost.
