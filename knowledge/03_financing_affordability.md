# Financing and Affordability Guide

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. Figures are illustrative planning assumptions, not lender quotes.

## Affordability rule of thumb

Demo Realty's planning rule is that the monthly home loan payment should stay at or below 35 percent of steady household income. Buyers with other loans should count those payments inside the same 35 percent.

## Down payment

Demo Realty assumes a planning down payment of 25 percent of the price. Lenders set the real minimum, and it depends on the applicant and the home.

## Interest rate and term

For early planning, use an illustrative annual rate of 14 percent over a 20-year term. Actual rates change often, so every estimate must be confirmed with a lender before a buyer relies on it.

## Budget for extra costs

Buyers should keep aside an extra 6 percent of the price for transfer costs, legal fees, inspection and moving. A home priced exactly at the top of the budget therefore leaves no room for these costs.

## Older homes

Lenders may offer shorter terms or lower amounts on homes more than 30 years old, or on homes that need major repair. Buyers considering such a home should speak to the lender before making an offer.
