# Property Inspection Guide

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. An inspection does not replace a professional structural survey.

## Structure and damp

Look for cracks wider than a coin along walls and ceilings, damp patches, peeling paint near skirting, and water stains around roof slabs. Visit after rain where possible, since leaks are easier to see.

## Plumbing and electrical

Run taps and check water pressure, look under sinks for leaks, and ask when the wiring was last replaced. Homes built before 1990 often have older wiring unless a rewiring record exists.

## Older and renovated homes

For homes older than 30 years, or where the seller reports past repairs, Demo Realty advises a paid structural survey before an offer becomes binding. Ask for receipts for any renovation that is mentioned in the listing.

## Apartments

Check the condition of lifts and water tanks, the backup power arrangement, parking allocation, and how the building manages maintenance funds.

## Homes that need renovation

Get at least two contractor quotes before making an offer on a home that needs renovation. Add a 20 percent contingency to those quotes when comparing the home against move-in-ready alternatives.
