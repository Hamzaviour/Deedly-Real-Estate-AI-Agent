# Home Buying Process Guide

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. Not legal or financial advice.

## Stages of a purchase

A typical purchase moves through six stages: setting a budget, shortlisting homes, viewing, making an offer, due diligence, and transfer of ownership. Buyers should not pay a deposit until title verification has started.

## Making an offer

An offer states the price, the deposit, the proposed transfer date, and any conditions. Common conditions are a satisfactory inspection, clear title, and financing approval. Demo Realty advisers only pass on offers that the buyer has confirmed in writing. The agent software cannot make or accept an offer.

## Deposits

Demo Realty recommends a deposit of no more than 10 percent of the agreed price, paid only against a signed sale agreement. The agreement should state when the deposit is refundable, for example if the title check fails or financing is refused.

## Timeline

A cash purchase usually takes four to six weeks from accepted offer to transfer. A financed purchase usually takes eight to twelve weeks, because the lender runs its own valuation and title review.

## When a listing changes

Listings can change status at any time. If a home goes under offer or is sold, the buyer should return to the shortlist and compare the remaining homes against the same criteria rather than raising the budget under time pressure.
