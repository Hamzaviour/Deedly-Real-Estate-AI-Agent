# Property Documents Glossary

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty. Not legal advice.

## Fard

The fard (fard-e-malkiat) is the revenue record extract showing who owns a piece of land and how much. A lawyer checks that the seller's name on the fard matches the sale documents.

## Registry and intiqal

The registry is the registered sale deed. The intiqal (mutation) updates the revenue record to the new owner's name. A purchase is not complete until both are done, or until the society transfer is done in societies that keep their own records.

## Allotment and transfer letters

In a housing society, the allotment letter shows that a plot was allocated to an owner. The society transfer letter records the change of ownership to the buyer.

## Sale agreement

The sale agreement (iqrar nama) records the price, deposit, payment schedule, transfer date and conditions. Demo Realty recommends paying a deposit only against a signed sale agreement that says when the deposit is refundable.

## Power of attorney

A general power of attorney lets another person act for the owner. Purchases through a power of attorney carry higher fraud risk, so the title checklist treats them as a warning sign.
