# Measurements: Marla and Kanal

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty.

## Marla

Plot sizes in Lahore are usually quoted in marla. Demo Realty listings use 1 marla = 225 square feet, the convention used by most housing societies in Lahore. Older revenue records use a larger marla of about 272 square feet, so always check which marla a document uses.

## Kanal

1 kanal = 20 marla. Using the 225 square feet marla, 1 kanal is 4,500 square feet. A 10 marla plot is therefore half a kanal, about 2,250 square feet.

## Plot size versus covered area

The marla size is the plot area. A house can have more covered area than the plot when it has two or more storeys, and an apartment is usually quoted by its covered area in square feet. Listing price per square foot in this course uses the listed area in square feet.

## Common sizes

Typical house plots are 5, 7, 10 marla and 1 kanal. 5 marla homes usually have 2 or 3 bedrooms, 10 marla homes 4 bedrooms, and 1 kanal homes 5 or more bedrooms. Larger homes on 2 to 4 kanal usually have 6 or 7 bedrooms, a basement and servant quarters. Farmhouses on the city edge are sold by kanal too, usually 2 to 10 kanal (9,000 to 45,000 square feet), and most of that is open land such as lawns and orchards, so the price per square foot is much lower than in town.
