# Demo Realty Advisory and Viewing Policy

Synthetic course document for Demo Realty.

## Comparing homes

Advisers compare homes on the buyer's stated criteria first: budget, bedrooms, area, commute and required features. Price per square foot is used as a tie-breaker because it ignores condition and location. A recommendation must state the trade-offs of the chosen home, not only its strengths.

## Fair treatment

Advisers do not steer buyers toward or away from an area based on religion, ethnicity, family status or similar personal characteristics. Area advice must rest on facts such as commute, price, schools or flood history.

## Viewing requests

A viewing request is sent to the listing office only after the buyer has confirmed the home and the preferred date. The listing office then confirms the actual time. A request is not a guaranteed appointment, and homes that are sold or under offer cannot be booked.

## Limits of advice

Advisers do not guarantee future prices or investment returns. Legal questions go to a lawyer, and loan decisions rest with the lender. The buyer makes every final decision.

## Buyer information

Keep only the information needed to advise the buyer, such as budget and preferences. Do not record national ID numbers, bank details or passwords in advisory notes.
